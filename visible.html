MAKE IT VISIBLE

Make invisible work visible.

Turn everyday planning and responsibilities into something people can recognize, share, and discuss.

Notice · Plan · Share
01

Name the whole task

Instead of writing “dinner,” include planning, checking ingredients, shopping, cooking, serving, and cleanup.

02

Assign ownership

One person should not have to repeatedly remind another. The owner notices, plans, does, and follows up.

03

Use a shared system

A calendar, checklist, or weekly board can make deadlines, appointments, and recurring work visible to everyone.

04

Review, don't assume

Schedules and capacities change. Check whether the arrangement still feels fair and realistic.

CONVERSATION STARTERS

Talk about responsibility without reducing it to blame.

“What am I currently remembering for everyone?”

Useful for identifying reminders and coordination that are easy to miss.

“Which task can you own from start to finish?”

Moves the conversation from occasional help to full responsibility.

“What would make this arrangement more sustainable?”

Recognizes that time, money, health, and schedules can affect what is possible.

“Fairness becomes easier to discuss when the work becomes visible.”
