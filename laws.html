Laws & policies

These selected Philippine laws are included for educational orientation. This page is not legal advice.

R.A. 9710

Magna Carta of Women

Addresses women's rights, substantive equality, and protection against discrimination.

Official text ↗
R.A. 11313

Safe Spaces Act

Addresses gender-based sexual harassment in public, online, workplace, and educational settings.

Official text ↗
R.A. 11210

Expanded Maternity Leave Act

Provides longer maternity leave and recognizes the recovery and caregiving responsibilities that follow childbirth.

Official text ↗
R.A. 8187

Paternity Leave Act

Gives qualified fathers paid leave to support their spouse and newborn child, helping make early care a shared responsibility.

Official text ↗
R.A. 8972 / R.A. 11861

Solo Parents Welfare Act

Provides support and benefits for solo parents who carry caregiving, household, and financial responsibilities.

Updated law ↗
R.A. 9262

Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act

Recognizes physical, sexual, psychological, and economic abuse and provides legal remedies for women and children.

Official text ↗
R.A. 11058

Occupational Safety and Health Standards Law

Requires safer workplaces and supports the right of workers to protection from preventable hazards.

Official text ↗
How these connect to mental load: Leave, safety, anti-discrimination, and family-support policies can reduce the pressure placed on one person to absorb care work alone. Check the official text and current implementing rules for details.
