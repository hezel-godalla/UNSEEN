What Is the
Mental Load?

Mental load is the invisible work of keeping everyday life running: noticing what needs attention, remembering details, making decisions, planning ahead, and checking that things actually get done.

In simple terms: it is the work happening in your mind before, during, and after the visible task.

What happens behind a task?

VISIBLE WORK

What people can easily see

Cooking dinner, buying groceries, cleaning a room, attending an appointment, or taking someone to school.

+
INVISIBLE WORK

What may happen in the background

Noticing, remembering, researching, deciding, scheduling, anticipating needs, reminding others, and following up.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Helping is not always the same as owning the responsibility.

If one person has to notice a task, decide what should happen, ask someone else to help, and later check whether it was completed, that person may still be carrying much of the mental load even when the visible task is shared.
