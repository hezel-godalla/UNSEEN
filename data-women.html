THE DATA · WOMEN

Women and unpaid care.

Time-use research makes some of this unequal workload visible, even though the experience differs across countries and households.

76.2%

of total unpaid care-work hours were performed by women

ILO analysis of time-use data from 64 countries found women performed more than three times as much unpaid care work as men.

Women 76.2% Men 23.8%*

*Calculated as the remaining share; percentages are rounded. ILO analysis covered 64 countries.

WHAT IT MEANS

Care is time, attention, and responsibility.

Unpaid care includes direct care for people as well as domestic work such as cooking, cleaning, and household management. The time behind these tasks can limit rest, education, and paid work.

READ WITH CARE

A global pattern is not every person’s story.

The 76.2% figure summarizes data across countries included in the analysis. It is not a prediction for every household, and it does not capture all differences within the category “women.”

Source International Labour Organization, “Care work and care jobs for the future of decent work” (2018) Based on time-use data from 64 countries. The report states women performed 76.2% of total unpaid care-work hours.
