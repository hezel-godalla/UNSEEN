About UNSEEN

UNSEEN is a student-led educational concept designed to raise awareness about overlooked work that keeps homes, schools, and communities running.

Our advocacy

Recognize invisible labor, question stereotypes, and encourage shared responsibility.

Our approach

Combine explainers, activities, data literacy, Philippine context, and practical action.

How to read the website's numbers

Statistics are labeled by source, year, and scope. Global figures should not be presented as if they describe every Filipino household. The 5-point displays on the Women and Men pages are visual conversions of the cited percentages, not scientific ratings of people or genders.
